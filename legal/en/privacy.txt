PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: October 11, 2026 — Version 1.2

Hop 'n Bop ("the Game") is published by Snoring Cat LLC ("we", "us", "our").
This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.


1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

- Account information: When you sign in through a third-party provider the
  Game offers (currently Google or Facebook), we receive your display name,
  profile image URL, and a provider-specific user ID. The Game does not ask
  sign-in providers for your email address, and we do not keep it. If an
  earlier version of the Game received your email address from Google, it
  is deleted the next time you sign in, or sooner. We do not receive or
  store your password.
- Device identifier: If you play anonymously, we generate a random device ID
  stored locally on your device. When you use online features, we also store
  it on our servers to identify your account.
- Gameplay data: Match results, scores, kills, deaths, and other in-game
  statistics.
- Social data and settings: Your friends, blocked players, parties, party
  chat messages, players you recently played with, online status, in-game
  notifications (such as friend requests and the details of matches you
  join), and settings synced across your devices.
- IP address: We receive your IP address when the Game connects to our
  servers or to a game server. When you look for a match, we store it with
  your account to choose a game server near you, and delete it within two
  hours.
- Technical data: Game version, platform, operating system, and crash
  reports. Crash reports contain error messages and excerpts from the Game's
  logs, with sign-in tokens and account identifiers removed. We do not store
  your player ID, IP address, or device ID with crash reports.
- Consent records: When you accept our Terms and Privacy Policy, the Game
  saves the date and time and the legal document version you accepted. This
  record is stored only on your device, not on our servers, and is removed
  when you sign out or your account is deleted.


2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

- Authenticate your identity and maintain your account.
- Track gameplay statistics and display leaderboards.
- Show your display name and profile image to other players in the Game.
- Provide friends, parties, party chat, online status, and settings sync.
- Match you with other players and choose a game server near you.
- Diagnose crashes and improve the Game.
- Keep our servers secure and working.


3. INFORMATION SHARING

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as
described below, with service providers that run parts of the Game for us and
with the sign-in providers you choose to use. We comply with the EU General
Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act
(CCPA).

- Other players and the public: Other players see your display name,
  profile image, and online status in the Game. Public leaderboards, in the
  Game and at hopnbop.net/leaderboard, show anyone, without signing in, each
  listed player's rank, display name, and best single-match score of all
  time. They never show player IDs, usernames, profile images, device IDs,
  IP addresses, email addresses, when a score was set, or other match
  statistics. Only accounts with a display name are listed: guest accounts
  have none and never appear, and banned accounts and accounts being deleted
  are left out. If you signed in with Google or Facebook, your display name
  is the name on that account's profile, which may be your real name, and it
  is the name shown on these public leaderboards.
- Hosting: Our backend runs on a cloud server from Hetzner Online GmbH in
  Hillsboro, Oregon, United States. That server stores your account and game
  data, including your sign-in IDs, friends, online status, settings,
  statistics, leaderboard entries, and party chat messages.
- Game servers: Edgegap runs a game server for each match in a data center
  chosen to be near its players, which may be outside your country. Edgegap
  receives the players' IP addresses to choose the location, and their player
  IDs so the server knows who may join. If everyone in a match is in the
  United States or Canada, the match may run on our own server instead. We
  check this with a DB-IP country database on our server, so your IP address
  is not sent to anyone else for that check. During a match, the game server
  receives your IP address, player ID, display name, profile image URL, and
  gameplay data.
- Website and backups: Cloudflare hosts our website and the browser version
  of the Game, and sees visitors' IP addresses. It also stores our nightly
  database backups. For Google sign-in, a small service we run on Cloudflare
  exchanges Google's one-time code for your sign-in token, and stores
  neither.
- Sign-in: Google and Facebook are contacted when you sign in or link an
  account, and receive no gameplay data from us. When the Game shows a
  profile image, it loads the image from Google's or Facebook's servers,
  which can see the IP address of the device loading it.


4. DATA RETENTION

Gameplay data is retained for the lifetime of your account. Consent records
stay on your device until you sign out or your account is deleted. Your
stored IP address is deleted within two hours of being stored.

When you delete your account, we immediately delete your profile details
(display name, profile image URL, any email address we hold, language,
location, and time zone), your stored IP address, friend code, friends and
blocked players, parties, party chat messages, notifications, leaderboard
entries, match history, settings, and other game data, and remove you from
other players' recent players lists. The public leaderboard at
hopnbop.net/leaderboard can take up to about a minute to stop showing you,
or longer only while our database is down. We keep only what lets you sign
back in to cancel the deletion: your sign-in IDs (your device ID and the IDs
of the providers you signed in with). If you cancel, you keep your account, but the
data we deleted does not come back. Otherwise, your account and sign-in IDs
are permanently deleted after 30 days, along with anything you add if you
keep playing in the meantime. Deleted data can remain in our database backups
for up to 8 days after it is deleted. Crash reports are deleted after 30
days. Server logs, which can include IP addresses, are kept only as long as
needed for security and troubleshooting.


5. YOUR RIGHTS

- Access: Request a copy of your data via in-game settings or email.
- Portability/export: Download all the data we store about you, in JSON
  format, via in-game settings (Settings > Account > Export My Data). The
  export holds your account details and sign-in IDs, any email address we
  hold, your stored IP address, friend code, friends and blocked players,
  parties, party chat messages, notifications, leaderboard entries, match
  history, players you recently played with, online status, settings, and
  whether your account is scheduled for deletion. Crash reports are not
  linked to your account, so they cannot be included.
- Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data via in-game settings
  or email. See our Data Deletion Policy.
- Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data via email.
- Restriction: Request we limit processing of your data.
- Objection: Object to processing of your data.
- Do not sell: We do not sell personal information (CCPA).

To exercise any right, use the in-game Account settings or email
privacy@hopnbop.net.

CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS: Under the CCPA, you have the right to know what data
we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information.
We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email
privacy@hopnbop.net or use the in-game Account settings.


6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly
collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected
data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.


7. SECURITY

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for our website, for sign-in,
and for data sent between the Game and our backend server. Gameplay traffic
during a match is not always encrypted. Our database backups are encrypted at
rest. Access to player data is restricted to authorized systems.


8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the
top will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the Game after
changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
