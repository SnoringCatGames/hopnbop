Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Version 1.2

You have the right to request deletion of all data associated with your Hop 'n Bop account.

How to Delete Your Data

Before deleting your account, you may export your data via Settings > Account > Export My Data.

Open Settings in the game, go to Account, and select "Delete Account". Your account will be scheduled for deletion, and you have 30 days to cancel by signing back in. After that, the deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you are unable to access the game, email privacy@hopnbop.net with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your player ID so we can locate your account.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently delete:

  • Your player profile (display name, player ID, profile image URL).
  • All linked authentication provider mappings, and your email address if you signed in with Google.
  • Gameplay statistics and match history.
  • Cloud-synced settings.
  • Friend connections (removed from both sides).
  • Party memberships, online status, and the list of players you recently played with.
  • Leaderboard entries.
  • Your stored IP address.
  • The consent record stored on your device.

Timeline

When you delete your account in the game, your display name is removed from your public profile and your friends, leaderboard entries, match history, settings, and other game data are deleted immediately. The rest of your account, including your sign-in IDs, device ID, email address, and original display name, is permanently deleted 30 days later unless you sign back in to cancel. Email requests are processed within 30 days. Deleted data can remain in our database backups for up to 8 days after it is deleted. Once your account is permanently deleted, it cannot be recovered.

Data We Cannot Delete

Aggregated, anonymized analytics data (e.g., total matches played across all players) does not contain personally identifiable information and is not subject to deletion.
