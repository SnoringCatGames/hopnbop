Patch Notes

v0.55.0

October 2026

Bunny names everywhere, and tougher matches.

  • Players now go by the bunny name they spawn with everywhere: in the lobby, during matches, and on the results screen.
  • Leaderboards and Recent Players show the bunny name from the match that earned the score.
  • Account display names are now generated, never taken from a sign-in provider.
  • More reliable match endings and reconnects: dropped players no longer hold up results, and Play Again keeps your party together.
  • Session security improvements.

v0.54.0

October 2026

Longer matches, player stats, and stability.

  • Matches now run a full 3 minutes.
  • The in-game leaderboard now shows the same public rankings as the website.
  • New My Stats screen: your best score, rank, and recent matches.
  • Automatic crash reporting so bugs get fixed faster.
  • Smoother sign-in, sign-out, and session handling.
  • Security hardening and privacy improvements.
  • Updated Terms and Privacy Policy (you will be asked to review and accept them).

v0.7.2

March 2026

Friends, parties, and leaderboards.

  • Friends system. Add and remove friends using friend codes.
  • Parties. Group up with friends and matchmake together.
  • Global and weekly leaderboards with per-level filtering.
  • Profile images from login providers shown on leaderboards.
  • Cloud sync for settings across devices.
  • Redesigned settings menu with account, friends, and info panels.
  • New icons throughout the UI.

v0.6.0

March 2026

Web build and cross-play support.

  • Play directly at hopnbop.net (web build).
  • Cross-play between web and native clients.
  • Platform-aware matchmaking (prefers same platform, relaxes after 15 seconds).
  • Website with leaderboard, blog, and legal pages.
  • Discord community link in settings.

v0.5.1

March 2026

Initial public release.

  • Multiplayer with rollback netcode.
  • Matchmaking via AWS GameLift FlexMatch.
  • Google and Facebook authentication.
  • Anonymous play with device-based accounts.
  • Web build with WebSocket transport.
