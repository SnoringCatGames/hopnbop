Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Version 1.2

Hop 'n Bop ("the Game") is published by Snoring Cat LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in via Google, Facebook, Steam, Epic, Apple, or Game Center, we receive your display name, profile image URL, and a provider-specific user ID. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your email address and store it with your account. We do not receive or store your password.
  • Device identifier: If you play anonymously, we generate a random device ID stored locally on your device. When you use online features, we also store it on our servers to identify your account.
  • Gameplay data: Match results, scores, kills, deaths, and other in-game statistics.
  • Social data and settings: Your friends, blocked players, parties, players you recently played with, online status, and settings synced across your devices.
  • IP address: We receive your IP address when the Game connects to our servers or to a game server. When you look for a match, we store it with your account, replacing any earlier one, and use it to choose a game server near you.
  • Technical data: Game version, platform, operating system, and crash reports. Crash reports contain error messages and excerpts from the Game's logs, with sign-in tokens and account identifiers removed. We do not store your player ID, IP address, or device ID with crash reports.
  • Consent records: Timestamp and legal document version of your agreement to our Terms and Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your account.
  • Track gameplay statistics, display leaderboards, and show your profile image to other players in-game and on leaderboards.
  • Provide friends, parties, online status, and settings sync.
  • Match you with other players and choose a game server near you.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the Game.
  • Keep our servers secure and working.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as described below, with service providers that run parts of the Game for us and with the sign-in providers you choose to use. We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

  • Hosting: Our backend runs on a cloud server from Hetzner Online GmbH in Hillsboro, Oregon, United States. That server stores your account and game data, including your sign-in IDs, friends, online status, settings, statistics, and leaderboard entries.
  • Game servers: Edgegap runs a game server for each match in a data center chosen to be near its players, which may be outside your country. Edgegap receives the players' IP addresses to choose the location, and their player IDs so the server knows who may join. If everyone in a match is in the United States or Canada, the match may run on our own server instead. We check this with a DB-IP country database on our server, so your IP address is not sent to anyone else for that check. During a match, the game server receives your IP address, player ID, display name, profile image URL, and gameplay data.
  • Website and backups: Cloudflare hosts our website and the browser version of the Game, and sees visitors' IP addresses. It also stores our nightly database backups. For Google sign-in, a small service we run on Cloudflare exchanges Google's one-time code for your sign-in token, and stores neither.
  • Sign-in: Google and Facebook are contacted when you sign in or link an account, and receive no gameplay data from us. When the Game shows a profile image, it loads the image from Google's or Facebook's servers, which can see the IP address of the device loading it.

4. Data Retention

Gameplay data is retained for the lifetime of your account. Consent records are retained for 3 years after account deletion for legal compliance. When you delete your account, we immediately remove your display name from your public profile and delete your friends, leaderboard entries, match history, settings, and other game data. We keep the rest of your account, including your sign-in IDs, device ID, email address, and original display name, for 30 days so you can cancel the deletion by signing back in. After that, your account is permanently deleted. Deleted data can remain in our database backups for up to 8 days after it is deleted. Crash reports are deleted after 30 days. Server logs, which can include IP addresses, are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your data via in-game settings or email.
  • Portability/export: Download your data in JSON format via in-game settings.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data via in-game settings or email. See our Data Deletion Policy.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data via email.
  • Restriction: Request we limit processing of your data.
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data.
  • Do not sell: We do not sell personal information (CCPA).

To exercise any right, use the in-game Account settings or email privacy@hopnbop.net.

California Residents

Under the CCPA, you have the right to know what data we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email privacy@hopnbop.net or use the in-game Account settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for our website, for sign-in, and for data sent between the Game and our backend server. Gameplay traffic during a match is not always encrypted. Our database backups are encrypted at rest. Access to player data is restricted to authorized systems.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
